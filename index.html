Interactive Recycling Directory

Quickly search common materials to learn exactly how and where to recycle them in your community.

PETE 1 / HDPE 2

Rigid Plastics

Highly Recyclable

Rinse thoroughly, flatten to save space, and put back original plastic caps before placing in your curbside bin.

PAP 20

Paper & Cardboard

Highly Recyclable

Flatten all boxes completely. Keep them completely dry and remove any heavy grease, tape, or plastic lining.

GL 70

Glass Containers

Infinitely Recyclable

Wash thoroughly. Most local centers accept clear, green, and amber glass jars and bottles. Do not mix with window glass.

E-WASTE

Electronics & Batteries

Safe Drop-off Only

Never throw electronics in standard trash. Please bring them to a local certified electronic waste collection center.
